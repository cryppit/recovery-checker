Cryppit · Recovery Checker Source

Did you send to a smart wallet on the wrong chain?

Paste the address and pick the chain the money landed on. We read the chains and tell you the honest answer: whether it can be recovered, and the risky steps if it can. Some losses — like Abstract Global Wallets — usually can't be recovered, and we say so.

Keyless · read-only · we never deploy or sign

What the answer can be

  • ⚠ Possibly recoverable a real path exists, with a deploy-race risk — we give the exact steps
  • ⚑ Occupied an address already has code there — verify who controls it
  • ✗ Very likely unrecoverable e.g. an Abstract Global Wallet — we say so plainly
  • ? Unknown we can't confirm it — never a guess, never false hope

What leaves your browser: to read the address, your browser asks each chain's public RPC service about it, so those services can see the address and your IP. The Abstract wallet archive is downloaded in small pieces. Nothing else leaves your browser — no account, no tracking, no cookies, nothing stored.

We never ask for a seed phrase, private key or OTP, and would refuse one if pasted. We hold no funds and never deploy or sign anything — any deployment you attempt, you do yourself, at your own risk.
